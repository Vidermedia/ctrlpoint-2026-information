## 4. Open qualifier conditions

**Confirmed CS2 event settings:** Free entry; 128 accepted teams per qualifier; five starters and up to two substitutes; two single-elimination qualifiers, BO1 through the Round of 32 and BO3 from the Round of 16; both semifinal winners qualify; the losing semifinalists play a BO3 reserve-order match. The four-team LAN uses BO3 semifinals and a BO3 final, with no third-place match. The CS2 prize pool is PKR 500,000, distributed 60% / 30% / 5% / 5%.

Every playing participant must hold Pakistani citizenship; dual citizenship does not remove eligibility where Pakistani citizenship remains valid. A foreign-owned organisation may register an otherwise eligible Pakistani roster, but a Pakistani organisation may not field an ineligible foreign player. Citizenship is not residence: this event does not add a Pakistan-residency requirement. Players must be able to use the published server region and attend the LAN. No player receives a special server or schedule concession based on their location.

Entries are accepted in the order of complete, eligible registration up to 128 per qualifier. Verification may finish after the cutoff but must apply objective published tests. Remaining eligible entries form a timestamp-ordered waiting list. No purchase, invitation or sponsor relationship grants entry priority.

The public registration ledger contains an entry ID, team name, player aliases / approved game identifiers, qualifier selection, original submission timestamp, material amendment timestamp, completeness timestamp, order and acceptance status. Private identity evidence, birth dates, phone numbers and bank information are never included. First-come-first-served order must be publicly inspectable before registration opens and thereafter retained with history.

A single-elimination qualifier uses BO1 through the Round of 32 and BO3 from the Round of 16. The two semifinal winners qualify. The semifinal losers play one BO3 reserve-order match within the four-day window. Fewer than four eligible checked-in rosters in a qualifier requires an announced corrective decision; it does not authorise private invitations or fabricated qualifying matches.

## 6. Stages, LAN progression and replacements

Both qualifiers are online. The four-team main stage is played in person on an organiser-controlled LAN server. The approved LAN seeding gives semifinal pairings 1 versus 4 and 2 versus 3. Both semifinals and the final are BO3. There is no LAN third-place match; both semifinal losers share third–fourth place and equal prizes.

A withdrawal before the reserve cutoff is filled through the vacated qualifier's third-place roster, then fourth-place roster, then the other qualifier's third and fourth, excluding already-qualified or ineligible rosters. A reserve receives no preferential treatment because it is personally known to the operator. The complete sequence, acceptance clocks, multiple vacancies and late-withdrawal rules are binding in the rulebook. No replacement enters after the main stage starts.

The CS2 prize pool is PKR 500,000 and the placement distribution above is confirmed by the organiser. Qualifier and reserve-order matches have no separate cash prizes. No additional compensation is provided beyond the published prize pool and five-player team support: no appearance fees, MVP or participation bonuses, or revenue shares. Joint placements receive identical prize amounts.

Prizes are stated in PKR and are not contingent on a sponsor paying the operator. Prize money will be paid within **three calendar months of the conclusion of the LAN finals**, no later than **25 January 2027**. Teams must promptly provide accurate payee information and any required payment documents through the official private contact. Any legally required withholding must be identified and evidenced. No unexplained processing charge or discretionary fine is deducted. Actual paid prizes and forfeited awards must be recorded accurately.

The nominated payee must be authorised by the playing roster or by an evidenced team agreement. The operator will not decide undisclosed private splits. A genuine payee dispute affects only the disputed award and does not justify delaying everyone else's prizes. The full cancellation, disqualification and payment provisions appear in the CS2 rulebook.

## 10. Objective integrity and exclusion rules

Eligibility exclusions are limited to the published citizenship / age / account / roster conditions; active game- or tournament-applicable Valve, chosen-platform or ESIC suspensions; a binding CTRLPOINT suspension issued through the published disciplinary procedure; documented cheating, account sharing, match manipulation or material falsification; and unresolved prohibited conflicts of interest. An old unrelated-game ban or a vague rumour is not automatically treated as a current Counter-Strike competition ban.

Every exclusion identifies its rule and evidence basis, offers the stated appeal route and is recorded publicly with proportionate redaction. A protective suspension pending investigation must be identified as provisional, not as a finding of cheating. Rules apply equally regardless of the affected roster. Financial interests or operator / staff relationships with participants are disclosed and handled under Valve Section 5.3; this rulebook does not substitute for the required relationship disclosures and approvals.

Any relevant operator or staff relationship with a participant must be publicly disclosed and approved as required by Valve Section 5.3 before that participation is allowed. Public disclosures and decisions are recorded in [records/DECISIONS.md](records/DECISIONS.md).
